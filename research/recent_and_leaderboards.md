Compiled 2026-10-30 for the Ronaldo / Messi data-journalism site. Every line ends with its source URL and that source's date.

**Source tiers:**
- **FIFA:** official match data was read from FIFA's public API (`api.fifa.com/api/v3/calendar/matches?idCompetition=17&idSeason=285023` and `/api/v3/live/football/17/285023/{stage}/{match}`, the same IDs as the fifa.com match-centre links cited below). FIFA articles were read through FIFA's own content API (cxm-api.fifa.com).
- **Wikipedia:** list pages were read as raw wikitext on 2026-10-30. Each one is cited with its own "updated" stamp.
- **Unreachable:** uefa.com, fbref, Transfermarkt and iffhs.com could not be read from this network. UEFA and IFFHS figures are therefore second-hand and are flagged where used.

**Dates:** the "(source date)" is the publication or "as of" date of the source. Where only the URL carries a date, it is marked "URL-dated".

1. **Knockout goals (5e):** no authoritative list exists in reachable sources, and the two secondary lists conflict (see 5e).
2. **Ronaldo's total:** 985 per the SPL, ESPN and RonaldoStats versus 984 per Opta and others, depending on the 23 Oct 2026 disputed goal.

4. **Single-source claims:** Ronaldo as "oldest outfield starter" at a WC, and some Wikipedia-only honours (2025 Nations League Finals top scorer, Primeira Liga all-time award). These are flagged above.
5. **UEFA and IFFHS:** pages were unreachable, so their figures above are second-hand.
